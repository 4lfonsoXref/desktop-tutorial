Our Story

External Reference works from a clear premise: design is a process in constant evolution. Over two decades, the studio’s multidisciplinary team has grown into a practice with a distinct language that combines architecture, digital tools, and crafts, with experiential design and contemporary luxury at its core.

Each project is an opportunity to innovate and transfer the knowledge gained to future work. This approach allows us to integrate cutting-edge technologies into the creation of memorable and highly sophisticated spaces.

In projects such as the restaurant CAAA, in Lucerne, Switzerland, the Spectacular Suites at Hotel W and the waterfront Escaleras y Mirador both in Barcelona, we introduced AI and generative tools into the design and 3D modelling workflow, and Augmented Reality to connect the

computational vision and built form with a high level of control and precision.

This technical expertise extends from the development of advanced material and engineering solutions to architectural innovation and adaptive urban planning, developed custom for highly demanding contexts. From the Pinko flagship store at Rome Fiumicino Airport, which led to the firm’s development and patenting of a custom fire-retardant coating that met strict airport regulations without compromising spatial quality or brand identity; to the combination of innovation, craftsmanship, and attention to detail that defines Plaza Mahou at the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium.

Escaleras y Mirador Vela, Barcelona, Spain (2022)

Leadership

Dr. Carmelo Zappulla is an Italian architect and founder of External Reference, a Barcelona-based architecture studio specializing in experiential retail and hospitality, corporate and residential architecture, urban design, world expos and pavilions, and product design. Holding a Ph.D. with honors from Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, Zappulla is renowned for integrating cutting-edge computational tools and parametric design with sustainable architecture.

His work reflects a “cradle to cradle” approach, ensuring complete material reuse within a circular economy. Under his leadership, External Reference has executed landmark projects such as the Spanish Pavilion at EXPO Dubai 2020, executive floors for Sberbank Moscow, and the immersive WOW concept store in Madrid.

Additionally, he has collaborated with Presentedby on award-winning retail spaces across major cities worldwide, where he also serves as Chief Creative Officer.

Zappulla’s projects have garnered numerous international accolades, including the Architecture Master Prize, INARCH and SBID Awards, London Design Awards, and the Ro Plastic Prize at Milan Design Week 2023. As an academic, he directs the Master in Experiential Design at LCI Barcelona and teaches at IAAC Barcelona. His work has been exhibited at the Venice Biennale and MAXXI Rome, and published in “Domus,” “Dezeen,” and “Blueprint.”

“We integrate luxury and sustainability, digital and physical, art and technology, adapting these cultural aspects to the needs of our clients in order to shape spaces that reflect both our design philosophy and the client’s unique individuality.”

Carmelo Zappulla

PhD Architect · CEO & Head of Design at External Reference
